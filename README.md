## ⚡ Quick Start

1. Enable **Allow Developer Console** under Options → Keyboard/Mouse.
2. Start a local campaign or host a game, then press `~` to open the console.
3. Enter `sv_cheats 1` to enable cheat commands where permitted.
4. Enter the commands listed below to activate individual cheats.

## ✨ Key Cheats

### 🛡️ Survivor Cheats

- ❤️ **God Mode** — `god 1` makes the survivor team invulnerable.
- 🩸 **Buddha Mode** — `buddha 1` allows damage but prevents death.
- 🔫 **Infinite Ammo** — `sv_infinite_ammo 1` provides unlimited ammunition.
- 🧱 **Noclip** — `noclip` lets the player fly through walls and obstacles.

### 💣 Weapons and Upgrades

- 🎯 **Laser Sight** — `upgrade_add laser_sight` adds a laser sight.
- 🔥 **Incendiary Ammo** — `upgrade_add incendiary_ammo` applies incendiary ammunition.
- 💥 **Explosive Ammo** — `upgrade_add explosive_ammo` applies explosive ammunition.
- 🧰 **Weapon Spawning** — Use `give` with a weapon name to spawn equipment.

### 🧟 Infected and Horde Control

- 🚫 **Stop Common Infected Spawns** — `director_no_mobs 1` prevents mob rushes.
- 👹 **Spawn Special Infected** — `z_spawn hunter`, `z_spawn smoker`, or `z_spawn tank` creates an infected enemy.
- 🧟 **Stop Director** — `director_stop` stops director-controlled enemy spawning.
- 👁️ **Blind Infected** — `nb_blind 1` makes infected unable to see survivors.

### ⏱️ Movement and Time

- 🕒 **Slow Motion** — `host_timescale 0.5` slows the game.
- ⚡ **Fast Motion** — `host_timescale 2` speeds up the game.
- 👥 **Control a Survivor Bot** — `sb_takecontrol nick` switches control to Nick.
- 🎥 **Third-Person Camera** — `thirdpersonshoulder` switches to an over-the-shoulder view.
